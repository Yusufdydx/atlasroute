<h1>AtlasRoute MCP — Healthcare Facility Location for Everyone</h1>

<p>In a world where healthcare facilities exist but aren't easily discoverable, AtlasRoute bridges the gap between people and the care they need. Whether it's an emergency on a remote highway, a tourist in an unfamiliar city, or an elderly person needing urgent care — AtlasRoute helps locate the nearest healthcare facilities when every minute matters.</p>

---

<h2>Understanding the Problem</h2>

<p>Healthcare facilities exist in every community, but the challenge lies in discovering them quickly, especially in unfamiliar areas or urgent situations. The traditional approach — searching Google Maps, asking locals, or driving aimlessly — wastes precious time that could be spent getting actual care.</p>

<p>Consider the reality: a driver experiences chest pain on a highway, a tourist cuts themselves badly in a remote area, or an elderly person feels chest tightness late at night. In these moments, the question isn't "which hospital is best" but simply "where is the nearest one?"</p>

<p>This is the problem AtlasRoute solves: helping anyone, anywhere, quickly discover nearby healthcare facilities.</p>

<h2>Who Benefits from AtlasRoute</h2>

<p>AtlasRoute serves anyone who needs to find healthcare facilities quickly. Our eight primary use cases represent real scenarios affecting millions of Americans daily:</p>

<h3>1. 🚨 Emergency Accident Cases</h3>
<p>Individuals involved in road accidents or sudden injuries who need to quickly surface the nearest accessible hospitals or clinics. Whether it's a highway collision, workplace injury, or home accident — AtlasRoute helps locate the closest appropriate facility fast.</p>

<h3>2. 👵 Elderly or Vulnerable Individuals</h3>
<p>Elderly users who may forget nearby hospital locations and need assistance identifying healthcare facilities when experiencing sudden illness or discomfort. Many seniors live alone and need a simple way to find care without asking neighbors or family.</p>

<h3>3. 🚖 Mobility Workers (Drivers, Delivery, Logistics)</h3>
<p>Workers who spend long hours on the road — truck drivers, delivery personnel, rideshare drivers — who may need nearby healthcare access due to fatigue, minor injuries, or sudden illness while in transit. They're often in unfamiliar areas and need quick answers.</p>

<h3>4. 🏕️ Tourists and Foreign Visitors</h3>
<p>Users unfamiliar with local infrastructure who need fast discovery of nearby hospitals or clinics in an unknown environment. Language barriers and cultural differences make finding care especially challenging for international visitors.</p>

<h3>5. 🧑‍🎓 Students in New Environments</h3>
<p>Students living in new cities or on campuses who may need to locate nearby medical facilities during non-critical health situations. College students especially may be new to an area and unfamiliar with local healthcare options.</p>

<h3>6. 🏗️ Field and Remote Workers</h3>
<p>Workers operating in construction sites, industrial zones, or remote areas where quick identification of the nearest medical facility is important for safety preparedness. Oil field workers, miners, and construction crews often work hours from the nearest hospital.</p>

<h3>7. 🚨 Disaster or Disruption Scenarios</h3>
<p>Situations such as floods, road blockages, or local emergencies where users need to identify functioning nearby medical facilities despite infrastructure limitations. When roads close or emergencies overwhelm nearby hospitals, knowing alternatives is critical.</p>

<h3>8. 🌍 Underserved or Rural Populations</h3>
<p>Users in areas with limited healthcare awareness or infrastructure who benefit from easier discovery of nearby medical facilities they may not know exist. Many rural communities have healthcare options residents simply aren't aware of.</p>

<h2>How AtlasRoute Works</h2>

<p>AtlasRoute provides a comprehensive suite of tools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), enabling AI agents to help users find healthcare facilities intelligently:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Find Hospitals</strong> — Locate nearby hospitals with route directions and travel time estimates</li>
  <li><strong>Find Pharmacies</strong> — Discover pharmacies for medication needs, including 24-hour locations when available</li>
  <li><strong>Find Clinics</strong> — Search for primary care clinics, urgent care, and general practice offices</li>
  <li><strong>Find Dentists</strong> — Locate dental offices, including those offering emergency dental services</li>
  <li><strong>Find All Facilities</strong> — Comprehensive search across all healthcare facility types at once</li>
  <li><strong>Route Calculation</strong> — Get turn-by-turn directions to any facility</li>
  <li><strong>Distance Calculation</strong> — Understand proximity and travel time before deciding</li>
  <li><strong>Symptom Triage</strong> — Assess urgency level to help users understand if they need emergency, urgent, or routine care</li>
  <li><strong>Patient Context</strong> — For users with FHIR health records, consider allergies and medical history in recommendations</li>
</ul>

<div class="highlight">
  <p><strong>Technical Foundation:</strong> AtlasRoute uses established open standards — Nominatim and OpenStreetMap for geocoding and facility data, OpenRouteService for routing, and HL7 FHIR for healthcare data integration. These are publicly available, globally-used standards already supporting millions of applications.</p>
</div>
